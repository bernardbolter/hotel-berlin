What this changes, and why

  • Drops the filled peach panel — matches how Rooms handles the identical job (teaser copy + one CTA to a full page) rather than the site having two different visual languages for the same kind of section.
  • One CTA, not two — "Visit Lütze" and "Reserve a table" going to different destinations (the full page vs. an OpenTable booking) is real functionality worth keeping, but as two competing buttons of equal visual weight in a small teaser block, it dilutes both. Your copy only asked for one link ("Go to Food and Drinks") — if reservation is still wanted from the homepage, it can live as a secondary text link near the CTA rather than a second button, or just live on the full Eat & Drink page instead where it belongs alongside actual hours and menu info.
  • Image goes arch-topped, matching Rooms — and I'd alternate the side it sits on from Rooms (which has image-left/text-right) to keep some rhythm across the homepage rather than every teaser looking identical in structure.
  • Hours/status badge dropped from this block — "Bar 10:00–open end" etc. is real, useful info, but belongs on the destination page (or the `/here` guest hub, where a checked-in guest actually needs it in the moment) rather than in a homepage teaser whose only job is to get a prospect to click through.
