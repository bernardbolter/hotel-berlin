Section Background Rhythm

Right now every content section between the dark hero, work, and footer blocks sits on plain white — the page reads as a hard flip from dark → white → dark → white. This keeps every dark section exactly as is and gives the white sections a family of light brand-colour tints, so the light stretch of the page has its own quiet rhythm instead of one flat white.

Neutral#F4F6F7 (existing bg-subtle)
Amber Light#FEF5EA
Coral Light#FEEFEF
Gold Light#F9F2EA
Green Light#E7EAE6
Teal Light#EBF3F5 (existing token)

Each tint is the same ~10% mix of the matching brand colour into white that produced the existing --teal-light token — so the new tints read as one family with what's already in DESIGN.md, not a new system.
